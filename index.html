Privacy Policy

English 한국어

1. Information stored locally by MORNING STAGE

MORNING STAGE is an alarm app. Information you enter or create for normal app operation, such as alarm times, alarm names, repeat days, mission selections, difficulty choices, and snooze preferences, is stored locally on your device.

MORNING STAGE does not currently require a user account and does not operate an ODYSSEY MAKERS server for uploading or remotely storing this alarm data.

2. Analytics

MORNING STAGE uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used, measure product performance, and improve the user experience.

Google Analytics for Firebase may automatically process information such as:

  • app-instance and device identifiers;
  • device model, operating system, and other device information;
  • general or approximate geographic information;
  • app launches, sessions, screen views, and other app lifecycle events; and
  • interactions with App features and events.

MORNING STAGE also records product-related analytics events such as alarm creation, alarm activation, mission start and completion, snooze use, and alarm dismissal. These events may include non-personal parameters such as mission type, mission difficulty, repeat-alarm status, mission completion time, or snooze duration.

MORNING STAGE does not intentionally include alarm names or other free-form alarm text in these analytics events.

Information processed through Google Analytics for Firebase is handled under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google Analytics for Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/products/analytics
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Advertising and third-party data processing

MORNING STAGE uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertising.

According to Google's documentation, the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share data such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and advertising interactions, including app launches, taps, and ad interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information; and
  • device or account identifiers, including Android advertising ID and App Set ID where applicable.

Google states that these data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's own policies and terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by applicable law or platform policy, MORNING STAGE uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising-related privacy choices before requesting ads.

When Google makes a privacy-options entry point available for your region and consent state, MORNING STAGE may provide an in-app way to reopen those options.

You may also be able to reset or delete your Android advertising ID through Android privacy or advertising settings.

5. App permissions

MORNING STAGE may use Android permissions and system capabilities needed for reliable alarm operation, including:

  • notification permission;
  • exact alarm scheduling;
  • full-screen alarm presentation;
  • foreground service operation;
  • restart or boot-completion handling; and
  • vibration.

The current Android version of MORNING STAGE does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for its core alarm functionality.

6. Sharing and sale of information

ODYSSEY MAKERS does not operate a user-account database for MORNING STAGE and does not sell locally stored alarm data.

Information may be processed by Google in connection with Google Analytics for Firebase, AdMob, and related Google services as described above. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Alarm and preference data stored locally by MORNING STAGE remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to Android system behavior.

Because MORNING STAGE does not currently provide user accounts or an ODYSSEY MAKERS cloud database for alarm data, ODYSSEY MAKERS generally does not possess a remote copy of that local alarm data.

Data processed by Google through Google Analytics for Firebase, AdMob, or other Google services is retained and deleted according to Google's applicable policies and user controls.

8. Security

MORNING STAGE is designed to keep core alarm and preference data primarily on the device.

Information transmitted to Google services such as Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using secure transport technologies such as HTTPS or TLS, according to Google's documentation.

Third-party providers apply their own technical and organizational security measures to information they process.

9. Children

MORNING STAGE is a general-purpose alarm app and is not specifically designed or marketed as a service for children.

If our target-audience configuration or applicable obligations change, we will update our practices and this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

10. International processing

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live, subject to Google's applicable safeguards and policies.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MORNING STAGE, third-party services, or legal or platform requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
