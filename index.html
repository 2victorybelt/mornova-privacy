Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026 · Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MORNOVA (the “App”), provided by Odysseyworks (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the App. If you have questions about this policy or privacy matters, contact mornova.app@gmail.com.

1. Information stored by MORNOVA

MORNOVA is an alarm app. Information you enter for normal app operation—such as alarm times, alarm names, repeat days, mission selections, difficulty choices, and snooze preferences—is stored locally on your device.

MORNOVA does not require an account and does not operate its own server for uploading this alarm data. If you place personal information in an alarm name, that information remains part of the local alarm data on your device unless another service described below processes separate device or advertising data.

2. Advertising and third-party data processing

MORNOVA uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertising. Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information; and
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where applicable.

Google states that these types of data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data handled by Google is subject to Google's own policies and terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by applicable law, MORNOVA uses Google's consent-management tools to present privacy choices before requesting ads. Depending on your region and choices, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads.

You may also be able to reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your device's Android privacy or advertising settings.

4. App permissions

MORNOVA may request Android permissions that are necessary for alarm reliability, including:

  • notification permission;
  • exact alarm scheduling;
  • full-screen alarm presentation;
  • foreground service operation;
  • restart/boot completion handling; and
  • vibration.

The current Android version of MORNOVA does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for its core alarm functionality.

5. Sharing of information

MORNOVA does not operate a user-account database and does not sell locally stored alarm data. Information may be processed by Google in connection with AdMob as described above. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect legal rights, security, or users.

6. Data retention and deletion

Alarm and preference data stored locally by MORNOVA remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to Android system behavior.

Because MORNOVA does not currently provide user accounts or a developer-operated cloud database for alarm data, we generally do not possess a remote copy of that local data. Data collected by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's applicable policies and user controls.

For questions or requests concerning information controlled directly by MORNOVA, contact mornova.app@gmail.com.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the App's functionality. Local alarm data is primarily maintained on the device. Third-party services such as Google apply their own security measures to information they process.

8. Children

MORNOVA is not specifically directed to children under 13. If our target audience, advertising configuration, or legal obligations change, we will update our practices and this policy as needed.

9. International processing

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their applicable safeguards and policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
